## Brand & Style

The design system is engineered for municipal resilience, mission-critical infrastructure oversight, and high-velocity civic emergency dispatch. It blends tactical command center utility with civic clarity, providing municipal operators, hydrological engineers, and field disaster teams with an uncompromising, situational-awareness interface.

The visual style is **High-Precision Civic Operations & IoT Monitoring**:
- **Tactical Utility**: Structural order reigns supreme. High information density without visual clutter, prioritizing instantaneous scanability during monsoon crises.
- **Deep Slate Anchors**: Persistent naval slate surfaces anchor critical navigation, situational status, and AI monitoring feeds, projecting stability and institutional authority.
- **Precision Data Hierarchy**: Industrial, monospaced telemetry counters paired with geometric grotesque headings offer crisp, legible, and actionable instrumentation across wall-mounted incident consoles and ruggedized field tablets.

## Colors

The palette establishes a high-contrast division between mission command structural elements and tactical field telemetry. 

- **Primary (`#0E7C86`)**: Vibrant hydraulic teal. Applied to active surveillance modes, primary confirmation dispatches, interactive telemetry nodes, and system-ready states.
- **Secondary (`#0B132B` / `#0F1B2D`)**: Deep Naval Slate & Ink. Reserved for the persistent global command bar, terminal header bands, sensor stream frames, and high-elevation utility modules.
- **Canvas & Surface System**: Operational base canvas rests on `#F3F5F9`, supporting elevated pure-white cards (`#FFFFFF`) with cool slate architectural hairline borders (`#E1E6EE`).
- **Critical Severity 5 Crimson (`#B71C1C`)**: Reserved exclusively for flood surge alerts, structural blockage overflows, and critical triage actions. Supported by subtle triage tinted backgrounds (`#FDECEC`) and high-luminance AI vision bounding overlays (`#FF3B30`).
- **Severity 4 & Warning Amber (`#9A4A00`)**: Indicates capacity thresholds, rapid accumulation rate warnings, and urban compliance violations; paired with light amber containers (`#FFF1DC`).
- **Operational Forest Green (`#15803D`)**: Signifies active deployed crews, pumps operating at target volume, and verified clear drain corridors; paired with alert-cleared containers (`#DCFCE7`).
- **Muted Steel Slate (`#5B6B80`)**: Secondary labels, geospatial grid axes, inert sensor boundaries, and technical metadata.

## Typography

Typography enforces a strict operational separation between narrative civic context, structural interface anchors, and machine-generated data streams:

- **Space Grotesk (Display & Headlines)**: Delivers an authoritative, forward-engineered appearance for catchment names, ward indicators, module headers, and modal alerts.
- **Inter (Body Copy)**: Guarantees effortless scanability for incident dispatch briefs, meteorological narratives, and field maintenance instructions.
- **JetBrains Mono (Telemetry, Coordinates, & Identifiers)**: Governs all CCTV identifiers (`CAM-MUTHA-08`), geospatial coordinates (`18.5204° N, 73.8567° E`), ultrasonic culvert depth metrics (`+1.42m`), sensor timestamps, and risk score indices. Its fixed-width cadence prevents layout jitter during real-time data streaming.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system implements a dense 12-column responsive fluid grid structured for data-heavy monitoring and emergency response screens.

- **Grid Model**: A standard 12-column grid system paired with standard `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.5rem` (24px) outer container margins.
- **Master-Detail Split Architecture**: The primary monitoring view features a 60/40 or 70/30 spatial split on desktop screens:
  - **Master Panel (Col 1-7 or 1-8)**: High-altitude dynamic GIS flood map, active drain network topography, and automated CCTV feed grids.
  - **Detail Inspector (Col 8-12 or 9-12)**: Synchronized real-time telemetry card, risk score gauges, live video filmstrip inspection, and incident action dispatch bars.
- **Reflow & Breakpoints**:
  - **Desktop (>=1280px)**: Persistent naval command header, split-screen master-detail operational workbench, concurrent live video filmstrips.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: Master view stacks above detail inspector or switches via a toggle tab bar. Gutters tighten to `1rem`.
  - **Mobile (<768px)**: Single-column linear layout. Video filmstrips shift to horizontal touch carousels; action bars pin to the bottom viewport edge for field operation accessibility. Outer margin drops to `1rem`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages a hybrid model of crisp low-contrast surface outlines combined with subtle ambient shadows, optimizing visibility under bright emergency control room lighting:

- **Base Layer (Canvas)**: Non-reflective operational backdrop (`#F3F5F9`).
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Toolbars)**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a structural 1px border (`#E1E6EE`) and an ultra-subtle ambient drop shadow (`0 1px 3px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.05)`).
- **Tier 2 (Floating Controls & Popovers)**: Pinned filter toolbars, coordinate overlays, and floating map controls use white surfaces with elevated shadows (`0 4px 16px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.08)`) and matching borders.
- **Command Overlays & Vision HUDs**: Dark glass surfaces (`#0B132B` at 85% opacity with 8px backdrop blur) housing monochrome JetBrains Mono sensor legends, camera IDs, and live visual telemetry directly on streaming video feeds.
- **Urgent Elevation (Alert Banners & Overlays)**: Severity 5 states use a high-visibility Crimson drop border and a soft red ambient glow (`0 0 12px rgba(183, 28, 28, 0.25)`).

## Shapes

The interface balances tactical precision with clean readability:

- **Base Cards & Surfaces**: Standardized at `0.75rem` (12px) border radius to establish friendly yet disciplined boundary envelopes.
- **Telemetry Chips & Badges**: Set to `0.25rem` (4px) or full pill forms (`9999px`) for quick glance recognition.
- **AI Vision Bounding Boxes**: Strict `0px` sharp rectangular corners with 2px high-visibility contrasting stroke lines (`#FF3B30`), augmented by crosshair-style corner ticks for algorithmic clarity.
- **Action Buttons & Input Shells**: Set to `0.5rem` (8px) for comfortable touch targets during operational high-stress data entry.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Bars
- **Primary Dispatch Action**: Solid hydraulic teal (`#0E7C86`) fill, pure white text, 8px radius, medium weight. On hover: darkened teal (`#0A5D65`).
- **Emergency Escalation Button**: Solid crimson (`#B71C1C`) fill, pure white text, pulse highlight on Sev 5 activation.
- **Secondary / Action Tool**: Outlined button with 1px border (`#E1E6EE`), slate text (`#0B132B`), pure white background.
- **Bottom Incident Action Bar**: Sticky docked footer container on mobile/tablet; persistent docking card in desktop master-detail pane. Houses direct quick-action buttons: *Deploy Quick-Response Team*, *Engage De-watering Pumps*, *Trigger Public Siren / SMS Alert*.

### Badges & Telemetry Status Chips
- Built with `JetBrains Mono` at `label-sm` or `label-md`.
- **Severity 5 (Critical Overflow)**: Background `#FDECEC`, text `#B71C1C`, 1px solid border `#F87171`. Prefixed with flashing indicator dot.
- **Severity 4 (Warning / Choked)**: Background `#FFF1DC`, text `#9A4A00`, 1px solid border `#FDBA74`.
- **Severity Normal / Operational**: Background `#DCFCE7`, text `#15803D`, 1px solid border `#86EFAC`.
- **Offline / Sensor Error**: Background `#F3F5F9`, text `#5B6B80`, 1px dashed border `#CBD5E1`.

### Circular SVG Risk Meters
- Concentric ring visualization rendering instant risk scores (0–100%).
- Background track in `#E1E6EE`, progress arc stroked dynamically based on value: Teal (0–39%), Amber (40–74%), Crimson (75–100%).
- Center text: Prominent risk value in `Space Grotesk` (bold) stacked above subtext in `JetBrains Mono` displaying sensor rate-of-rise (e.g., `+12 cm/hr`).

### AI Vision Overlays & Multi-Frame Video Filmstrips
- **Surveillance Viewport**: Deep Naval Ink background (`#0B132B`) with live timestamp overlay in top-right corner.
- **Bounding Boxes**: Direct video vector layer rendering high-visibility red (`#FF3B30`) or amber (`#F59E0B`) bounding frames around detected bottlenecks (e.g., *PLASTIC DEBRIS CLOG 94%*, *SURCHARGE WATER LEVEL*).
- **Filmstrip Navigator**: Horizontal multi-frame sequential thumbnail strip positioned under the primary viewport. Features 5-second interval freeze frames with active scrubbing cursors and monospaced frame-delta timestamps (`-00:15s`, `-00:10s`, `-00:05s`, `LIVE`).

### Cards & Master-Detail Inspection Lists
- **Sensor List Cards**: White surface, 12px radius, hairline border (`#E1E6EE`). Hover reveals a 2px left border accent colored by sensor status.
- Left column hosts sensor ID (`JetBrains Mono`), Ward location (`Space Grotesk`), and operational chip. Right column displays culvert level progress bar and risk percentage.
- **Active Selection**: Selected card gains an outline of 2px solid `#0E7C86` with a subtle teal-tinted background (`#F0FDFA`).

### Form Controls & Filter Toolbars
- **Input Fields & Selectors**: 8px radius, white surface, 1px border (`#E1E6EE`), placeholder in `#5B6B80`. Focused state displays 2px border in primary teal with zero ambient glow.
- **Filter Toolbar**: Segmented pill toggles enabling instant filtration by Ward (e.g., *Kasba, Shivajinagar, Kothrud*), Severity Tier (1-5), and Drain Type (*Primary Nullah, Storm Pipe, Roadside Gully*).
